POPIA & Data Protection

How NAS technical measures support business data protection and governance objectives under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Supporting Security & Governance Objectives

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), responsible parties must implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to secure personal data. NAS managed cyber resilience services support these governance objectives through encrypted backups, access protection, vulnerability scanning, and security awareness training.

Operator Responsibilities

When NAS manages backup and security infrastructure for clients, we operate as an Operator processing data strictly in accordance with contractual requirements and security standards.

Compliance Disclaimer

Please note: NAS services support security and governance objectives, but do not automatically grant 100% legal POPIA compliance, as organizational policies, consent management, and legal governance remain the responsibility of each business entity.
