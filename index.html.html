FAQs

Common IT Questions Answered

We believe in keeping things simple. Here are the questions we hear most often.

Do you offer remote support or do you have to come on-site?

We offer both! Many issues can be resolved quickly and conveniently via remote support — meaning we can fix your problems without you or us having to travel anywhere. For hardware repairs, installations, and complex network setups, we'll send a technician to your location across Gauteng.

How quickly can you respond when I have an IT problem?

For clients on our monthly support plans, we aim to respond within 1–2 hours for urgent issues. Most remote problems are resolved the same day. On-site visits are typically scheduled within 24–48 hours depending on availability and urgency.

What's the difference between Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace?

Both give your business professional email and productivity tools. Microsoft 365 includes Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams — great for businesses already using Windows and Office software. Google Workspace includes Gmail, Docs, Drive, and Meet — ideal for collaboration and businesses that prefer web-based tools. We'll help you choose the best fit and set everything up for you.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No long-term lock-ins for our basic plans. We prefer to earn your business every month through great service. We do recommend at least a month-to-month agreement so we can plan your support effectively, but we're flexible and can discuss arrangements that suit your business.

Can you help with home office setups?

Absolutely! We support both businesses and home users. Whether you need WiFi set up, a computer repaired, or a home office fully kitted out with the right technology, we can help. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.

Is my data safe when you do remote support?

Yes, absolutely. We use industry-standard, secure remote access tools. You can see everything we're doing on your screen in real time, and you're always in control. We treat your data with the utmost confidentiality and never access anything beyond what's required to fix your issue.
