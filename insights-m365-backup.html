Why Microsoft 365 Alone Is Not a Backup (And What Gauteng Businesses Risk)

Most South African SMEs assume that moving email and files to Microsoft 365 means their data is automatically backed up. Here is why that assumption causes permanent data loss.

Moving from an on-premise server to Microsoft 365 gives businesses reliability, remote collaboration, and strong platform uptime. However, platform availability is not the same thing as an independent backup.

Microsoft's Shared Responsibility Model

Microsoft explicitly recommends in its Services Agreement that customers regularly back up their own content using third-party services. Microsoft's job is to keep Exchange Online, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Teams running. Your company's job is to protect and retain your own business data.

Key Risk: Default recycle bins in Microsoft 365 typically purge deleted items after 30 to 93 days. If an employee deletes critical client files or emails and nobody notices for four months, that data is permanently gone.

Four Common Ways Businesses Lose Microsoft 365 Data

  • Accidental or malicious deletion: When a staff member leaves and their licence is removed, their mailbox and OneDrive data can be permanently purged if not properly archived.
  • Ransomware syncing via OneDrive: If ransomware encrypts a workstation synced to OneDrive or SharePoint, those encrypted files can rapidly overwrite clean versions in the cloud.
  • Compromised email accounts: Attackers who hijack an M365 mailbox often create forwarding rules and permanently delete sent invoices or audit trails.
  • POPIA & dispute retention gaps: Standard M365 retention policies are not a substitute for immutable, searchable point-in-time backups when legal or audit disputes arise.

What Proper Microsoft 365 Backup Looks Like

Independent cloud-to-cloud backup stores encrypted copies of Exchange mailboxes, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Teams completely outside your Microsoft 365 tenant. With NAS's Managed Backup & Data Protection, backups run automatically, are monitored daily, and allow fast point-in-time restoration of single emails or entire folders.
