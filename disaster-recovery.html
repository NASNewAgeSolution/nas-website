Understanding The Difference

Backup vs. Disaster Recovery

Understanding why having backup files is different from having a operational disaster recovery plan.

Backup Focus

Backup helps protect recoverable copies of data and systems. However, a business with backup alone may still require replacement server hardware, complex software reconfigurations and long restoration download times before normal operations resume.

Suitable for day-to-day file recovery and non-critical system restoration.

Disaster Recovery Focus

Disaster recovery focuses on restoring critical workloads and business operations when normal infrastructure is completely unavailable or destroyed.

A properly configured disaster recovery solution provides an alternative cloud environment in which supported workloads can run while your primary environment is restored.

Disaster Recovery Framework

NAS DR Planning & Services

Structured recovery planning tailored to your critical system dependencies.

Readiness Assessment

Evaluate current infrastructure vulnerabilities, single points of failure, and operational risk exposure.

RPO & RTO Objectives

Define realistic Recovery Point Objectives (acceptable data loss) and Recovery Time Objectives (acceptable downtime) for each workload.

Recovery Planning

Design supported workload and cloud recovery-environment configurations aligned with business requirements.

Failover & Failback

Establish documented failover and failback procedures, where supported by configured infrastructure.

Recovery Testing

Conduct periodic recovery simulation testing where included in the agreed service scope to validate failover health.

Recovery Documentation

Maintain clear operational recovery documentation detailing step-by-step procedures during a major incident.

Important Recovery Considerations

Not every business needs a dedicated DR environment. The right solution depends on the criticality of your workloads, acceptable downtime, internet connectivity, and budget. NAS does not promise a specific recovery time until your environment has been assessed and validated.
