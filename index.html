Business Risk Reality

Your Business Depends on IT.
Is It Properly Protected?

Your team depends on email, business applications, devices and digital information every day. When those systems fail—or critical data becomes unavailable—the impact extends far beyond IT.

01 / Critical Exposure

Data Loss & Ransomware

Accidental deletion, hardware failure or a single ransomware incident can lock or erase the client records, financial files and operational data your business relies on to trade.

Primary Operational Risk for Growing SMEs
02 / Silent Gaps

Unclear Backup Protection

Many businesses assume their data is backed up—only to discover during an emergency that key folders or Microsoft 365 mailboxes were never included in the scope.

Requires Verified Recovery Testing
03 / Endpoint Drift

Cybersecurity Gaps

Outdated laptops, unpatched operating systems, weak configurations and limited security visibility leave staff devices exposed to avoidable phishing and malware risks.

Managed Endpoint & Human Layer
04 / Unplanned Downtime

IT Disruption Without a Recovery Plan

When systems fail, businesses need more than software—they need a clear support process, accountable oversight and a practical plan to restore normal operations.
