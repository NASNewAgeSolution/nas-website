FAQs

Common IT Questions Answered

We believe in keeping things simple. Here are the questions we hear most often.

Do you offer remote support or do you have to come on-site?

We offer both! Most issues can be resolved remotely, which is faster and more convenient. For hardware installations or complex issues, we can arrange on-site visits at your convenience.

How quickly can you respond when I have an IT problem?

For support plan members, we typically respond within 1-4 hours. For urgent issues, we can often help immediately via WhatsApp or phone. One-off customers usually see a response within 24 hours.

What's the difference between Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace?

Both are great cloud solutions. Microsoft 365 offers better integration with Windows and Office, while Google Workspace is simpler and more affordable. We can help you choose based on your needs and budget.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No long-term contracts required! You can cancel anytime, though we offer discounts for annual commitments if you prefer. We focus on earning your business every month.

Can you help with home office setups?

Absolutely! We help individuals and small businesses set up secure home offices with WiFi, VPNs, and backup solutions. We even offer remote troubleshooting for your home tech.

Is my data safe when you do remote support?

Yes! We use secure, encrypted remote access tools. Your data stays on your devices, and we never access anything without your permission. Your privacy and security are our top priority.
