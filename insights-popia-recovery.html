POPIA & Data Recovery: What South African Business Directors Need to Know

POPIA compliance is not just a privacy policy PDF on your website. Section 19 requires ongoing, reasonable technical safeguards—and directors are accountable when breaches happen.

Under Section 19 of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), every South African organisation must secure the integrity and confidentiality of personal information in its possession by taking appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures to prevent loss, damage, unauthorised destruction, or unlawful access.

What "Reasonable Technical Safeguards" Means in Practice

  • Encrypted, Verified Backups: Protecting against permanent loss or destruction of client, payroll, and supplier records.
  • Endpoint Data Loss Prevention (DLP): Controlling sensitive data movement to USB drives, personal cloud storage, and external email.
  • Device Encryption & Remote Wipe: Ensuring a stolen company laptop does not immediately turn into a reportable Information Regulator data breach.
  • Continuous Patching & Access Controls: Closing known vulnerabilities and ensuring ex-employees lose Microsoft 365 and server access immediately.
Why Recovery Testing Matters: Having a documented, tested Disaster Recovery Plan demonstrates due diligence to clients, insurers, and regulators—and drastically reduces downtime if an incident occurs.
