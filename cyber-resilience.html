Core Concept

What Is Cyber Resilience?

Cyber resilience is your business's ability to prepare for, withstand and recover from cyber incidents and IT disruption. It combines prevention, detection, response and recovery. NAS helps businesses bring these elements together through agreed backup, cybersecurity, monitoring and recovery services.

Our 5 Proposal Pillars

Our Cyber Resilience Pillars

Five integrated operational pillars — Protect, Prevent, Detect, Respond and Recover — that safeguard your business data, devices and operations.

PILLAR 01

PROTECT

Safeguard critical business data, workstations, servers and Microsoft 365 workloads through managed backup and access controls.

PILLAR 02

PREVENT

Reduce attack surface through endpoint security policies, anti-ransomware controls, patch management and staff security awareness.

PILLAR 03

DETECT

Identify security alerts, backup failures, system anomalies and configuration weaknesses early through proactive monitoring.

PILLAR 04

RESPOND

Act swiftly when issues arise through structured incident procedures, remote IT support, investigation and agreed escalation.

PILLAR 05

RECOVER

Restore protected files, mailboxes, workstations and servers efficiently through verified recovery processes and DR planning.

Why Cyber Resilience Matters

A business can have antivirus software and still lack a reliable recovery process. It can have backups and still be uncertain whether critical applications can be restored in time.

NAS looks beyond individual tools to understand how protection, recovery and business requirements fit together.

What Happens First?

Every engagement begins with understanding your environment. We review the relevant systems, identify potential gaps and recommend a practical approach based on your business needs.
